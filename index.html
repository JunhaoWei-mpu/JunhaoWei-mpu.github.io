About Me

I am a Research Assistant affiliated with the Faculty of Applied Sciences at Macao Polytechnic University. My research focuses on robotics and autonomous systems, with an emphasis on UAV path planning, trajectory generation, reinforcement learning, and Vision-Language-Action (VLA) systems.

My current interests include safe autonomous navigation, optimization-based planning, learning-based control, multimodal perception, and intelligent aerial manipulation. I am particularly interested in combining learning and geometric planning to make UAV systems more efficient, robust, and deployable in real-world environments.
